Elemental Interactions
Earth - High Damage, Physical, Stagger Damage
Fire - High Damages, DoTs
Water - Mostly Utility to combo, debuff removal, Healing
Air - Higher overall movespeed, Greater-air control
Frost - Slows, Shatter (Combo with Earth).
Lighting - High in-combat speed, Stuns
Pressure - Combo for damage, Super Hi-Jump / Push
Volcanic - Less damage than Fire, Slow projectiles, Less damage then Earth, Larger AoE
